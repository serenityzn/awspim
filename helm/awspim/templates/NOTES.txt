The pod assumes IAM permissions via the ServiceAccount
"{{ include "awspim.serviceAccountName" . }}".
Ensure the IAM role attached to this ServiceAccount has
the following permissions:

  secretsmanager:GetSecretValue   (pim/aws-accounts, pim/mfa-*)
  sqs:SendMessage                 (manager_sqs_arn)
  ses:SendEmail                   (if MFA is enabled)
  sts:AssumeRole                  (for generating temp creds)

Set the IAM role ARN in values:
  serviceAccount.annotations:
    eks.amazonaws.com/role-arn: arn:aws:iam::<ACCOUNT_ID>:role/<ROLE_NAME>
